# Cookie Clicker Grandmaster v6.1 (Firefox)

Autoplay for `orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker` (game v2.058), built for reaching 100% of the 622 achievements as fast as the game allows.

## Install
1. Firefox 128+ → `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` → **Load Temporary Add-on** → pick `manifest.json`.
2. Open Cookie Clicker. The panel sits bottom-left; **F8** pauses/resumes everything.
3. Temporary add-ons disappear when Firefox restarts (load it again, or sign it with `web-ext sign`).
4. v5 uses new storage keys. Your v4 on/off switches carry over; v4's tuning values do not.

## What limits 100%
- **Sugar lumps.** "Reach level 10" for all 20 buildings costs 1,100 lumps, plus 78 for cursor level 12 (needed for *Pyramid scheme*). At about one lump a day that would take years, so v5 makes nearly every lump **golden** (2–7 lumps each, see below). That is about 4x faster.
- **Cookie totals.** The last "ascend with N cookies baked" achievement needs 1 octodecillion (1e57) in one run, so it takes many ascensions.
- **Time-gated content.** Garden seeds, seasonal drops and 100 ascensions run in parallel with the above.

## How it plays
- **Purchases.** Every building and upgrade is tried virtually with the game's own CpS calculation and ranked by payback time. Buffs are excluded so a Click frenzy does not make mouse upgrades look amazing. A Lucky bank (6000× CpS) is kept. Cheap "Have N buildings" thresholds are topped up.
- **Lumps.** The next lump's type is decided by the exact millisecond you harvest. The bot scans the coming milliseconds with the game's own function and clicks on one that gives the type it wants. Missing types (bifurcated, caramelized, meaty) are targeted once each, then golden every time. At harvest it also briefly slots Rigidel (−1 h), switches the dragon to Dragon's Curve (−5%), and rounds the building count to a multiple of 10. Lumps go to the minigames first, then farm level 9 (full garden), then levels towards 10. With *Sugar baking* (+1% CpS per unspent lump, up to 100) it keeps 100 lumps after the minigame levels and only spends the extra ones; with *Sugar crystal cookies* (+1% CpS per building type at level 10) the whole road to level 10 is counted. *Sugar frenzy* is still used once a run.
- **Grimoire.** The next two Force the Hand of Fate outcomes are predicted before casting. Good ones are stacked on buffs; when the next two are both stacking buffs it **double-casts** (casts from a full bar, sells wizard towers so the leftover magic covers a second cast, casts again, buys the towers back). A predicted backfire is turned into a success with Diminish Ineptitude when that works, otherwise skipped with the cheapest spell predicted to succeed. Elder frenzy (×666) backfires are kept.
- **Pantheon.** Every layout of Godzamok, Mokalsium, Muridal, Selebrak, Cyclius and Jeremy is tried on the game's own CpS calculation and golden-cookie timer, plus Godzamok's measured combo income. Ascending empties the temple, and swaps come back slowly (1 h at 2 left, 4 h at 1, 16 h at 0), so only layouts reachable with the swaps in hand are compared: an empty slot is filled for +1% even with the last swap, replacing a god needs +3% and keeps one swap for Rigidel, and a god is only taken out when the slot is better off empty.
- **Godzamok combo.** Sells cheap buildings and buys them back in the same frame: +1% click power per building for 10 s, with no CpS lost. It runs whenever the extra clicks are worth 2× the sell loss (*Advanced → Godzamok*) (measured clicks/s × click value over the next 10 s, with Click frenzy and other buffs counted until they end). Grandmas and minigame buildings are never sold.
- **Dragon.** Levels are trained when rebuying the sacrificed buildings is cheap. All remaining levels (100 of one building each, then 50 and 200 of every building: Dragon cookie and the second aura slot) are trained mid-run in one go as soon as the game's own numbers say either (a) income with the two best auras and all those buildings gone is at least today's income, or (b) the bank can rebuy everything sacrificed and the stronger auras repay that within an hour; the Dragon row shows both numbers while it waits. The dragon menu stays open while the bot pets the dragon; its sacrifice button is the game's own and does not need pressing. During a click buff (Click frenzy, Dragonflight…) it may swap an aura for the buff's length when the extra income is at least 3× the two buildings the swap and swap-back cost. Auras are chosen by the same scoring as the Pantheon, now including Reality Bending and Dragon Guts (scored with the wrinkler income it adds, from the game's own eating and payout formulas; cookies held by wrinklers count at half because they are only spendable once popped). A change of aura needs +2%; an empty slot is filled for any gain. With *Pet the dragon* it pets the dragon (muted) until all four drops are found.
- **Garden.** A mutation planner works through every seed. It also fills all 36 tiles once (*In the garden of Eden*) and sacrifices the seed log when it is complete (+10 lumps, *Seedless to nay* the first time). After that, if the game's numbers say it pays, it runs a Whiskerbloom field on clay (+milk), harvesting each plant just before it dies (mature harvests count for the harvest achievements); otherwise it rediscovers the seeds and sacrifices again for another 10 lumps. Juicy Queenbeet farming is not used: the plot is cleared on every ascension and a Juicy Queenbeet takes 2–4 days to mature. Harvest-for-cookies plants are not farmed either: a Bakeberry costs 45 min of CpS and returns at most 30 min, a Duketater costs 8 h and returns at most 2 h, and Golden clover costs about 2 h of CpS per plant.
- **Stock market.** Buys at ≤ 0.6–0.75× the resting price (looser as brokers cut the fee), never while the price is falling. Sells on the first fast fall above cost, or at 2.5× the resting price. It only uses cookies above the Lucky bank. While buildings pay back within an hour it only buys very deep discounts, with half the budget. Everything is sold before ascending. Right before ascending, the bank (worthless after the reset) buys the stock achievements it covers: *Buy buy buy* and *Make my day* (one lot worth a day of CpS), *Full warehouses*, *Rookie numbers*, *No nobility in poverty*; they are sold again a market tick later.
- **Wrinklers.** Popped with real clicks on the wrinkler (as a player would). Kept attached normally; one that has been attached for 30+ min is popped (at most once per 20 min) when its cookies let the optimizer buy something that pays back within the hour and would otherwise take 10+ min of saving. Also popped for Halloween/Easter drops once they have fed for *dropPopMin* minutes (Balanced 10, Achievements preset 0), and right before ascending. *Moistburster* (200 pops) counts pops in one run only, so it is done as one sprint: once Elder Pact is bought, no pledge chain is under way and the game's spawn odds say it takes 2 h or less (needs Unholy bait), every wrinkler is popped on sight until 200, with ascension and pledges held meanwhile. Outside the sprint the Wrinklers row says why they are kept. Elder Pledges (5 in one run, for Elder slumber, once ever) make the game itself pop every wrinkler; the next pledge is bought within 3 s of the last one ending, before new wrinklers reach the cookie. Dragon Guts is scored for the run's steady state (pledges no longer make it look worthless), and combo aura swaps never remove it while its 2 extra wrinkler slots are in use.
- **Hunters.** Covers: Just wrong, Tiny cookie, Here you go, Tabloid addiction, ticker fortunes (O Fortuna), Olden days, a gift to yourself (No time like the present), Wrinkler poker, the full Grandmapocalypse chain (One mind's Yes/No prompt, Communal brainsweep, Elder Pact) and the pledge/covenant chain, Debt evasion (through the visible loan button), Eldeer, Fading luck, Third-party (registered through the game's mod API), Sugar frenzy, the season planner and Santa. The season planner only chases seasons whose achievements are still open, the one with the FEWEST drops missing first (every collection resets on ascension, so the closest one is finished first; most-missing-first used to send every run to Easter), stays until its drops are found, then settles in Christmas. With *Hunt seasonal drops* off (Fastest growth preset) it goes straight to Christmas. Santa is levelled every run (his level resets on ascension). *What's in a name* is done by clicking the bakery name and confirming it unchanged. Neverclick and Hardcore are off (they only count on Born again runs).
- **Auto-ascend.** Ascends when this run's prestige growth falls below its own average rate (restart overhead included). Bursts (cookie chains, storms) are capped so one lucky spike cannot trigger an early ascension, and cookies held by wrinklers count. It also waits for the Grandmapocalypse research chain to finish, for the 5 Elder Pledges of Elder slumber once they have started (the count resets on ascension), and for a started Moistburster sprint. Once the pre-ascend sequence has started it is not restarted by golden-cookie buffs, wrinklers that attach during the Lucky wait are popped first, and a failing backup is retried twice before it ascends anyway (with a notification). It holds while a new garden plant is growing, while the $10M stock profit is close, while the last few Easter eggs are missing, and during buffs. Before ascending it sells all stocks, pops wrinklers, opens the Chocolate egg (+5% of the bank), trains the dragon, spends the bank on count achievements, takes a loan (Debt evasion) and saves a backup. If *Lucky digit / number / payout* can be bought next, it waits for a prestige level containing one / two / four 7s (its low digits change with every cookie; the demand drops after 10, 20 and 30 min). Then it buys heavenly upgrades in priority order (saving chips for a listed upgrade within 4× reach) and fills permanent upgrade slots.

- **Golden & wrath cookies.** Every golden cookie is clicked, wrath cookies too (after the Grandmapocalypse almost all are wrath). Golden-cookie upgrades (Lucky day, Serendipity, Get lucky…) are valued as a share of income instead of waiting until they cost 1% of the bank.

## Panel
- **Overview** — what it is doing now, achievement progress, and the few achievements that need you.
- **Purchases** — everything the bot spent on (buildings, upgrades, heavenly, lump levels, market trades, dragon, Santa). Kept across reloads; filter by kind; **Export CSV**.
- **Log** — activity.
- **Settings** — three presets (*Fastest growth*, *Balanced* = recommended, *Achievements*), folding sections with a one-line hint per switch, a ★ on anything changed from the preset and *Reset to recommended*. Numbers are under *Advanced*. Version 6.1 sets every playing switch to Balanced once; later changes are kept. A **safety guard** checks CpS three minutes after a 6.1 feature acts (mid-run dragon training, combo auras) and switches that feature off after two drops of more than 10%.
- **Graphs** (button or F10) — a separate window you can drag, resize and expand: CpS, prestige and pending chips, achievements, lumps, bank vs wrinklers, with 1h–7d ranges and ascension markers. One point a minute, kept across reloads.
- The panel can be dragged by its header (double-click resets it).

## Needs you (3 achievements)
Cookie-dunker (short window), Stifling the press (narrow window, then click the ticker), Baby it's old outside (click an elf grandma at Christmas).

## Console
`CCA.todo()` lists remaining achievements. `CCA.diag()` shows API checks and per-task timings. `CCA.restore()` loads the newest backup. `CCA.purchasesCSV()` returns the purchase log as CSV.

## Rules it keeps
Only things a player can do: clicks, purchases, minigame buttons, the game's own dialogs, timing. It never calls `Game.Win` or edits counters. Wrinklers are popped with real clicks. It also reads the game's own random state to predict spells, lump types and stock trends (prediction only; nothing is changed).
